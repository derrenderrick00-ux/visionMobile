Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking

How do I schedule a ride or equipment rental?

Book directly through our online form above, or call us at +1 (678) 598-2974. Same-day bookings available 24/7.

Do you deliver equipment to my location?

Yes. We deliver wheelchairs, scooters, Hoyer lifts, hospital beds, and all equipment directly to your home, hotel, or hospital anywhere in our service area.

Are your vehicles wheelchair accessible?

Yes. All transportation vehicles are fully ADA compliant with ramps, lifts, and secure tie-down systems for all wheelchair types.

What rental periods are available?

We offer daily, weekly, and monthly rental options for all equipment. We also offer flexible extensions — just contact us before your rental ends.

Can I book for someone else?

Absolutely. Caregivers and family members can book on behalf of a patient or loved one. Include the recipient's name and any special requirements in the notes field.

Do you operate on weekends and holidays?

Yes — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year including all public holidays. Mobility needs don't follow a schedule, and neither do we.
