Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services

How do I schedule a ride?

You can schedule directly through our online booking form on this page, or call us at +1 (678) 598-2974. Most bookings are confirmed within 15 minutes during business hours.

Are vehicles wheelchair accessible?

Yes, all transportation vehicles are fully ADA compliant with wheelchair lifts, securement straps, and ample interior space for all wheelchair types including power chairs.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 24 hours or more in advance receive a full refund. Same-day cancellations may incur a $25 fee. No-shows are charged the full ride amount.

Can a family member or caregiver ride along?

Absolutely. One companion rides free with each passenger. Additional companions may ride for a small fee depending on vehicle capacity. Please mention this when booking.

Do you accept insurance or Medicare?

We work with most major insurance providers and can provide documentation for Medicare reimbursement. Contact us with your insurance details for verification.

What are your typical wait times?

Pre-scheduled rides have zero wait time—we arrive at your requested time. For same-day bookings, we typically arrive within 45-90 minutes depending on your location and traffic.

Are your drivers trained for medical transport?

All drivers undergo background checks, CPR certification, sensitivity training for passengers with disabilities, and defensive driving courses. Many have EMT or CNA backgrounds.

Do you accommodate service animals?

Yes, service animals are always welcome at no additional charge per ADA regulations. Please inform us when booking so we can prepare the appropriate vehicle space.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, cash, and most HSA/FSA cards. Payment is processed securely after your ride is completed or at equipment pickup.

Do you offer equipment delivery?

Yes, we deliver mobility scooters and wheelchairs directly to your home, hospital, or hotel. Setup and basic training on equipment use is included with every delivery.
