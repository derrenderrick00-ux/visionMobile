Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking

How do I schedule a ride?

You can book directly through our online booking form above, or call us at +1 (678) 598-2974. Same-day bookings are available subject to availability.

Are your vehicles wheelchair accessible?

Yes. All our transportation vehicles are fully ADA compliant and equipped with ramps, lifts, and secure tie-down systems for all wheelchair types.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance for guaranteed availability. However, we do our best to accommodate same-day requests — just give us a call.

Can I book for someone else?

Absolutely. Caregivers and family members can book on behalf of a patient or loved one. Just include the passenger's name and any special requirements in the notes field.

What areas do you serve?

We currently serve Seattle, Tacoma, Bellevue, Redmond, and surrounding King and Pierce County areas. Contact us if you're unsure whether your location is covered.

Do you operate on weekends and holidays?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week including public holidays. Mobility needs don't follow a schedule — and neither do we.
